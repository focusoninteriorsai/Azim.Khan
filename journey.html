2017THE COMPANY

FocusOn Interiors is founded.

Raja Khan and Shanu Khan establish FocusOn in Gurugram as an interior fit-out practice. It is the company Azim would join in its early years and grow alongside.

COMPANY ROOTS
2017 — 18CAREER

Starts at the operational back-end.

Azim joins FocusOn as a Backend Operator, learning procurement, coordination and site delivery close to the ground—where project details become daily decisions.

BACKEND OPERATOR
2020 — 21RESILIENCE

Keeps delivery moving through the pandemic.

FocusOn continues healthcare work at Sanar Hospital in Gurugram during the Covid-19 lockdown, with site teams working under safety protocols and coordination adapting to the moment.

SANAR HOSPITAL · GURUGRAM
OPERATIONSGROWTH

Grows into Head of Operations.

As FocusOn evolves into a delivery-led design-and-build firm, Azim takes on broader responsibility across teams, project coordination and execution. The work includes landmark spaces for L&T Finance, Luminous, Ericsson and more.

HEAD OF OPERATIONS
DECEMBER 2025COMPANY

FocusOn merges with Riya Enterprises.

The merger brings additional NBFC fit-out experience into the FocusOn platform under one identity, setting the stage for a wider national footprint.

A BIGGER PLATFORM
JANUARY 2026LEADERSHIP

Appointed Chief Executive Officer.

After more than eight years with the company, Azim becomes CEO of FocusOn Interiors, with a mandate to grow the business while protecting its delivery culture.

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER
2026 →WHAT’S NEXT

Azim and the FocusOn team are expanding capabilities and partnerships across India—from Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Bengaluru to Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kochi, Ahmedabad and beyond.
