About Azim Khan

Grown inside the delivery.Leading it nationwide.

Azim Khan is the Chief Executive Officer of FocusOn Interiors Decorators Pvt. Ltd. — a PAN-India design-and-build firm for workplaces and industrial environments. He is based in New Delhi.

Official press photograph© FocusOn Interiors

Biography

One company, every rung.

Azim Khan’s career has been built inside a single company. He joined FocusOn Interiors — founded in Gurugram in 2017 by Raja Khan and Shanu Khan as a small interior fit-out set-up — in its formative years, starting as a Backend Operator.

The back-end is where fit-out projects are won or lost: procurement, coordination between drawings and site, and the discipline of handing spaces over on time. Azim learned the business there and rose to lead it as Head of Operations, as FocusOn grew into what Realty+ describes as a PAN-India solutions provider — a transformation the publication credits him with shaping.

Along the way he worked on projects for organisations including Infosys, L&T Finance and Luminous, building long-term partnerships through transparency and reliability. In January 2026, following FocusOn’s acquisition and merger with Riya Enterprises — a firm known for NBFC-sector projects — he was appointed Chief Executive Officer, after more than eight years with the company.

“I am truly delighted to lead FocusOn Interiors at this exciting stage. Together with our talented team, we will create future-ready, innovative interiors for workplaces and industrial environments, while expanding our presence across India.”— Azim Khan, Realty+, 27 January 2026 ↗

Work philosophy

Design intent only matters when it translates into seamless execution on the ground.

The idea runs through every account of his work — and through the operating standard of the company he leads. FocusOn Interiors governs every engagement with four non-negotiables, known internally as C.Q.E.T.

Association with FocusOn Interiors

Spaces styled, stories told.
