01 A LEADER WHO GREW UP INSIDE DELIVERY

Eight years, one company,
every rung.

Azim’s career has grown alongside FocusOn Interiors—from the operational back-end to the responsibility of leading the business.

He joined FocusOn in its formative years as a Backend Operator, learning the practical foundations of fit-out work: procurement, coordination, site handovers and the everyday link between a drawing and a built space. He went on to lead operations as the company developed into a delivery-led design-and-build organisation.

In January 2026, after more than eight years with the company and its merger with Riya Enterprises, Azim was appointed Chief Executive Officer. He now leads FocusOn’s next phase of growth, with an emphasis on people, process and performance.
