Overview

The project

FocusOn Interiors delivered the fit-out of Emversity’s campus in Kochi, Kerala — an education environment built around learning and everyday work.

A clean, branded reception leads into flexible classrooms fitted with tablet-arm seating and linear lighting, alongside open workstations divided by green glass screens.

The project extends FocusOn’s delivery footprint to South India and into the education sector.
