02 THE STORY SO FAR

I learned the business
from the ground up.

The best way to understand a project is to be part of every step that brings it to life.

Azim Khan began his journey at FocusOn Interiors in the operational back-end—working across coordination, procurement and site delivery. Over eight-plus years, he grew into a leader who knows how to connect design intent with what happens on the ground.

After leading operations and helping deliver landmark workplaces for clients including L&T Finance, Luminous and Infosys, he was appointed Chief Executive Officer in January 2026. His focus now: build on the team’s strengths and take FocusOn’s delivery promise further across India.
